Introduction & About Us

Bridging Design Excellence & Practical Execution

Samniph Global Integrated Concept Ltd is a dynamic and forward-thinking construction and development company delivering integrated building solutions across residential, commercial, and mixed-use projects. We transform client ideas into functional, durable, and aesthetically refined developments.

Established to bridge the gap between design excellence and practical execution, the company has steadily grown into a trusted partner along the rapidly developing Lekki-Epe corridor in Lagos State—one of Nigeria’s most vibrant real estate growth zones.

Led by visionary professionals with practical experience in design, construction management, and real estate development, our leadership team drives strategic direction, operational excellence, regulatory compliance, and lasting partnerships.

Our Services

End-to-End Construction & Real Estate Value Chain

Every project benefits from coordinated planning, technical precision, cost efficiency, and quality execution from concept to handover.

01

Architectural & Structural Design

Concept development, space planning, working drawings, and structural detailing for residential homes, commercial complexes, and mixed-use developments—compliant with regulatory, safety, and environmental standards.

02

Construction Services

Full-scale building execution from site preparation and foundation works to structural framing, installations, and final finishing, backed by materials procurement, skilled labor coordination, quality assurance, and onsite supervision.

03

Project Management

Structured lifecycle oversight covering planning, scheduling, budgeting, risk management, procurement coordination, contractor supervision, and transparent reporting from initiation to final handover.

04

Property Sales

Strategic marketing and sales support for residential and commercial properties, including market analysis, pricing strategies, property presentations, negotiation management, and documentation facilitation.

05

Development Consultancy

Advisory services for individuals, investors, and organizations covering feasibility studies, cost estimation, project viability assessments, site analysis, development planning, and investment advisory.

06

Interior & Exterior Finishing

Space optimization, material selection, decorative finishes, ceiling works, and lighting coordination indoors, paired with facade treatments, painting, landscaping, and paving outdoors.
